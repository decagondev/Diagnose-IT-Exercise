# 01 — Exercise brief

## The slide (Gauntlet)
> **YOUR TURN — Don't solve it. Diagnose it.**
>
> **The problem:** "We need more AI workflows across the company, but most people don't know how to build them. The few engineers who do are completely bottlenecked — they can't keep up with demand. Everything is slowing down."
>
> **Your task:** Find the real constraint — not the symptom.
> **Ask:** Why does it matter that it's slow? Why has the team stopped using it?
> **Keep going:** Until you hit money, growth, or risk.

**Hand-in:** in under 5 minutes.

## What's actually being tested
| Skill | What good looks like | What fails |
|---|---|---|
| Separating claims from evidence | Splits the sentence into *observed* vs *believed* vs *goal* | Accepts "people don't know how to build" as the cause |
| Root-cause discipline | Keeps asking "so what?" until the answer is a cost | Stops at "engineers are busy" |
| Restraint | **No solution** in the hand-in | Pitches a platform, a training plan, or more hiring |
| Branching | Treats the two prompt questions as two separate branches | Merges "slow" and "stopped using" into one story |
| Honesty about data | Says which numbers are hypothetical and how to measure the real ones | Makes up stats and presents them as fact |
| Testability | Names the evidence that would confirm or kill the diagnosis | Gives a diagnosis nobody could prove wrong |

## Rules of the game
1. **Diagnose, don't solve.** A fix is allowed only as a hint about where to look next, never as the deliverable.
2. **Stop condition:** each chain ends at **money** (cost, revenue, cash), **growth** (scale, speed, market), or **risk** (security, compliance, key-person, reputation).
3. **The prompt is a hypothesis.** "We need more AI workflows" is a solution someone already picked. Treat it as a claim to test.
4. **Notice the hidden clue.** "Why has the team *stopped using it*?" implies workflows already exist and aren't being used. A good diagnosis picks up on that.

## Framing for a 5-minute hand-in
- 1 line: what the stated problem really is (a pre-chosen solution plus symptoms).
- 2 short chains: "why slow matters" and "why stopped using", each ending at money, growth, or risk.
- 1 sentence: the named constraint.
- 3 bullets: what evidence would confirm or kill it.
- No fix.
